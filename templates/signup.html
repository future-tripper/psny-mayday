Join the Crown

If there is one thing we're learning, it's that it is okay to not be okay. It is good to call out for help—for a friend, for a word, or a string of words like pearls, for something beautiful to connect us to one another in the face of sorrow.

The Poetry Society of New York invites you to answer the call: m'aider, come help me. Together, we'll weave a Crown—poets from around the world, writing between the lines of existing sonnets, creating an epic tapestry of interconnected verse. At PSNY, poetry is never a lonely artform. The more voices, the greater the Crown.
